About Us

Transparent, measured football predictions.

Professional Betting Tips publishes football predictions and, importantly, publishes how those predictions turn out.

How the predictions are made

  • Ratings: every team has a strength rating (Elo) and attack and defence scores, updated after every result.
  • Goals model: ratings are turned into expected goals, then into probabilities for a home win, draw or away win, over/under 2.5 goals and both teams to score.
  • Extra signals: bookmaker odds, ClubElo ratings and recent team news (injuries, suspensions) are blended in. Each prediction is also stored without them, so we can measure whether they help.
  • Self-learning: the model re-tunes itself as new results arrive.
  • No edits after kickoff: each prediction is locked before the match and graded against the real result.

What to expect

Football is unpredictable. Even good models are right about half the time on match outcomes, and long jackpot cards are very hard to get fully right. The Prediction Lab shows the live accuracy, including the misses.
